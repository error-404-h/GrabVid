Extract Audio from TikTok

📅 August 21, 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read 👤 GrabVid Team

Introduction

TikTok is full of amazing sounds and music. Sometimes you want to save just the audio without the video. GrabVid makes this process quick and easy.

Step 1: Copy the TikTok Video Link

Open TikTok, find the video with the audio you want, and copy its link from the share button.

Step 2: Paste the Link

Go to GrabVid and paste the link in the download box. Click the Download button.

Step 3: Choose Audio Format

From the available formats, select the audio option (MP3 or M4A) and your preferred quality.

Step 4: Download

Click the download button and save the audio file to your device. You can now use it offline.

Conclusion

Extracting audio from TikTok has never been easier. With GrabVid, you can save your favorite sounds and music in just a few clicks.
