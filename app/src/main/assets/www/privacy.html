Privacy Policy

📅 Last Updated: August 21, 2026 ⏱️ 10 min read

Welcome to GrabVid's Privacy Policy. This document explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our video and audio downloading service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. By using GrabVid, you agree to the terms outlined in this privacy policy. Please read this policy carefully to understand our views and practices regarding your personal data and how we will treat it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use GrabVid, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use our service. However, if you contact us for support, we may collect your email address and any other information you choose to provide.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information about how you interact with our website, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits. This helps us understand how users engage with our service and improve our offerings.
  • Video URLs: When you paste a video URL to download, we temporarily process this URL to retrieve the video information and generate download links. We do not store these URLs on our servers after the download process is complete.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze site traffic, and remember your preferences such as language and theme settings. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing and Improving Our Service: To process your download requests, deliver the videos and audio files you request, and continuously improve our service based on user behavior and feedback.
  • Analytics and Research: To analyze how users interact with our website, identify trends, and gather demographic information to improve our service and user experience.
  • Personalization: To remember your preferences such as language and theme choices so that we can provide a more personalized experience on your future visits.
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries, provide customer support, and send you important service-related announcements if necessary.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To protect our service, users, and infrastructure from unauthorized access, malicious activities, and security threats.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take data security seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security measures include:

  • Encryption of data transmitted between your browser and our servers using SSL/TLS technology.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments to identify and address potential risks.
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms to ensure that only authorized personnel have access to user data.
  • Data minimization practices, meaning we only collect and retain the information that is necessary for providing our service.

We do not store video URLs or downloaded content on our servers. All download processes are temporary and the data is deleted immediately after the download is completed. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

Our service may contain links to third-party websites, plugins, or services that are not operated or controlled by GrabVid. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit. Additionally, we may use third-party services such as analytics providers to help us understand how users interact with our service. These third-party providers may collect information about your use of our website through cookies and similar technologies.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When we no longer need your personal information, we will securely delete or anonymize it. In general, we retain usage data and analytics information for a period of up to 12 months, after which it is aggregated or anonymized. Video URLs are not stored at all and are immediately discarded after processing.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request restriction of processing your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive your personal information in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information for marketing purposes or on grounds relating to your particular situation.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: If we process your personal information based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided in the "Contact Us" section below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable laws.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

These countries may have data protection laws that are different from those in your country. However, we take steps to ensure that any such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and that your information remains protected. We implement appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to ensure that your personal information is adequately protected when transferred to third countries.
